## 1. What the Service is

BetterTavern is a tool for writing interactive fiction with AI: you import or make character cards, set up a scenario, and an AI model of your choice writes the story with you. The agent in the Workbench can also help you write character cards, look things up and make cover images.

To write stories and cards, you can use our official model right away, paid for with credits based on usage (see section 5), with nothing to set up; or you can enter your own API key in Settings to connect another model provider. The external services for web search, reading pages and generating images are connected by you, with your own API keys. The services you connect with your own API keys are called "your connected services" below.
